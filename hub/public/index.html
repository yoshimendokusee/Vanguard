Triage categories are START-style and come from a rescuer's spoken report, interpreted by keyword matching on a watch. They are advisory: confirm every patient on arrival. “Unassessed” means the report was saved but not understood. ETAs use the watch clock, which has no network time on an air-gapped LAN, so treat countdowns as approximate.
